# Lucentra AI Ltd — Outreach Email Template (v4 — cross-domain enthusiasm)

**For:** first-contact outreach to small / boutique app agencies.
**Tone:** confident, specific, direct, genuinely enthusiastic about the cross-domain moat. Vision-led. Filters out scope-cutters by stating the perfection bar up front. Joint voice — co-signed by both Lucentra co-founders.
**Engagement structure:** open — revenue-share or equity-share considered to minimise or remove upfront cash. Specific terms negotiated post-NDA.

## Notes on tone (for the sender — Clive)

1. **Specific reference to their work** — the `[ONE LINE FROM THEIR WEBSITE / PORTFOLIO]` line is critical. Spend 10 minutes on their site before sending. Reference a real project, not a generic compliment ("loved your work" is a tell of a mass mailer). Example good: *"the colour-grading work on the Hello Fresh redesign caught my eye"*. Example bad: *"impressive portfolio"*.

2. **Confident not desperate** — the framing is "we respect your work and Lucentra is interesting enough to be worth your time", not "please help us". A founder team with a working iOS build in TestFlight UAT, paid commercial infrastructure ready to activate (Stripe + Apple IAP), and three filed UK provisional patents (Bodhi 2026-04-08 + Lucentra app GB2611164.1 2026-05-13 + BMS GB2608130.7 2026-05-13), asking for delivery support, is a more attractive proposition than a founder pre-product looking to barter equity for unknown future value.

3. **Ten-day timer** — sets a soft deadline without being pushy. Most agencies have an internal "decide within X days" rhythm; ten days is enough to slot the read into their cycle without burying it.

4. **No percentages anywhere** — per the engagement-structure principle, all specifics are post-NDA. Do not mention 5% / 10% / X% revenue share or X% equity. The line is "revenue-share or equity that minimises or removes upfront cash" — that's the only commercial signal that goes in this email.

5. **Forwarded-introduction ask at the end** — this captures the value of the email even if the recipient says no. Agency partners tend to know other agency partners; the forwarded-intro path is often more productive than the direct send.

6. **Length** — keep the email under 350 words excluding signature. If you find yourself adding more, ask whether each sentence either (a) earns the read or (b) sets up the next step. If neither, cut.

7. **Subject line variants** — the default is professional and specific. Alternatives if you want to test:
   - *"Lucentra AI Ltd — exploring a delivery partnership"*  (default, recommended)
   - *"Quick question on agency partnership fit — Lucentra AI Ltd"*  (more casual)
   - *"Lucentra AI Ltd — first-contact note for [AGENCY NAME]"*  (most specific)

8. **Follow-up cadence** — if no reply by day 7, send ONE bump (one line: *"Hi [first name] — gently bumping the note above in case it slipped through. Happy to send a shorter version if useful. — Clive"*). After that, leave it alone. Repeated follow-ups burn the relationship.
