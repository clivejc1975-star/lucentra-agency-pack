# Lucentra — Functions Overview

**Format:** pillar-by-pillar feature summary at first-contact depth. Numbers are accurate at time of writing; feature counts grow weekly so treat as a floor not a ceiling. No per-feature internals — those are post-NDA.

**Top-level count:** 87+ named features across 6 life-pillars at the Pro+ tier. 4 paid tiers gate progressively richer subsets; the £19.99 Standard tier sees ~27 features (~31% of the surface), the £44.99 Pro+ tier sees all 87+.

## Pillar 1 — Health (longevity + body)

The largest pillar. Tracks the inputs that drive measurable lifespan: sleep, nutrition, movement, weight, biomarkers, cycle (for users who track it), supplements, illness risk, family medical history.

Representative features: longevity hero chart with projected years gained / at-risk · 29 evidence-based longevity protocols · wearable integration via Apple Health (iOS) and Health Connect (Android) — Garmin / Fitbit / Apple Watch / Oura / Whoop all sync through the OS aggregator · 2,145-item food database with photo logging + barcode scanning · supplement adherence engine · illness-risk predictor running cross-domain signals · biomarker tracking with reference ranges · sleep + recovery graphs · weight + body-composition tracking · fertility cycle tracking (when applicable) · medical-history + family-medical-history capture · symptom checker with AI triage (emergency keyword gate to 999/116-123/988 hotlines for serious indicators).

## Pillar 2 — Money (wealth + financial wellbeing)

Tracks financial health as a longevity input — money stress measurably reduces lifespan. Bank integration is live UK open-banking via TrueLayer (Pro+ only); 15 budget categories; recurring-transaction detection; net-worth tracking; financial-independence projection (FIRE methodology); savings goals; bills tracker; partner-and-deals surfacing (commercial revenue line).

Representative features: live UK open-banking sync (weekly Sunday cron) · AES-256-GCM at-rest token encryption · 15 budget categories with word-boundary classification · net-worth time series · savings goals · FIRE calculator with sex-specific Mifflin-St Jeor BMR plus 5 activity multipliers · markets / partners / deals surface · annual budget vs actual.

## Pillar 3 — Mind (mental health + reflection)

Daily emotional state tracking + 7 therapy modes + crisis-detection gate. Journaling with AI pattern analysis. Oracle reading deck (69 cards) for users who like that surface. Meditation library (24 unique sessions, 4 multi-day programmes, 56 total session-day slots). Sleep stories. Confession space (private, encrypted, optional).

Representative features: daily mood tracker with 1-10 scale + emoji + free-text · 7 therapy modes (CBT, ACT, IFS, gratitude, anxiety-targeted, depression-targeted, general) · journal with AI pattern analysis across entries · oracle reading deck · 24-session meditation library · 4 multi-day programmes · 8 ambient soundscapes · weekly truth letter from the chosen companion · sleep stories · confession space (one-way, encrypted) · cognitive games (15 games, brain-score gamification).

## Pillar 4 — Relationships

Tracks the social inputs to longevity — relationship quality, social isolation risk, family connection. Family profiles (up to 11 relationship types). Couples coaching as a separate add-on subscription (£79.99 / month per couple, distinct product). Confession space cross-listed.

Representative features: family profiles with relationship-type catalogue · couples coaching add-on (Maren AI couples coach, evidence-based Gottman + EFT + IBCT + NVC blend, separate £79.99 / month subscription) · social-isolation risk surfaced in cross-domain engine.

## Pillar 5 — Purpose (meaning + alignment)

Tracks long-term goals, values, and the gap between stated values and actual time-allocation. Annual letter (generated by chosen companion, personalised to user's current state). Weekly truth report. Value ledger. Goal contracts with accountability mechanism.

Representative features: annual letter generator (companion-voiced) · weekly truth report · value ledger · goal contracts with stake mechanism · meaning compass · alignment tracker.

## Pillar 6 — Career (work as a longevity input)

Tracks work-life integration, career trajectory, professional development. Career profile (23 capture questions). Cross-domain wiring into health, money, purpose pillars (career stress affects sleep affects mood affects money).

Representative features: career profile with 23-question capture · work → health / money / purpose cross-domain correlation · professional development goal alignment.

## Cross-cutting capabilities (run across all pillars)

- **Voice-first companion interface.** Tap the orb to talk. Speech-to-text, AI response, text-to-speech via Kokoro voices. 758+ voice command patterns recognised.
- **Cross-domain intelligence engines (24 engines).** Correlate signals across pillars rather than treating them as silos.
- **Persona consistency.** Whichever companion the user picked (Chloe, James, Flavio) appears across every pillar surface with their distinct voice.
- **Privacy + consent architecture.** GDPR Article 22 honoured; granular consent; revocable; 48-hour grace window on destructive actions like account delete.
- **Achievements + gamification.** 71 achievements wired into the longevity hero ring.
- **8 languages supported** for primary UI surfaces.
