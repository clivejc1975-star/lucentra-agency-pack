## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND NON-USE AGREEMENT

**Lucentra AI Ltd · `[COUNTERPARTY LEGAL NAME]`**

This mutual non-disclosure agreement is entered into as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` between:

**Lucentra AI Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **17174304** ("Lucentra"), and

**`[COUNTERPARTY LEGAL NAME]`**, of `[COUNTERPARTY ADDRESS]` ("Recipient"),

Together, the "Parties".

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential commercial relationship relating to Lucentra's AI-powered consumer platform and associated technologies (the "Purpose"). In connection with this, each Party may disclose Confidential Information.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public, proprietary, or commercially sensitive information disclosed by Lucentra, including but not limited to:

- Product architecture, systems, technical design and code
- AI models, pipelines, and workflows
- The system known as **"Bodhi"**, including its structure, logic, guardrails, and implementation
- Business plans, financials, forecasts, and pricing
- User data models, analytics, and growth strategies
- Designs, UX/UI, and product direction

Confidential Information includes information disclosed in written, oral, visual, or inferred form.

## 3. Non-Use and Non-Disclosure Obligations

The Recipient shall:

- Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose
- Not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent
- Restrict access internally to employees or contractors strictly on a need-to-know basis
- Apply at least the same level of protection as it uses for its own most sensitive information

## 4. Strict Non-Compete and Non-Derivation

The Recipient agrees that it shall not, directly or indirectly:

- Develop, build, advise on, or assist any product or service that is substantially similar to Lucentra or its underlying systems
- Replicate, reverse-engineer, or derive any system, architecture, or methodology disclosed, including Bodhi
- Use Confidential Information to inform work for any third party, including competitors, whether paid or unpaid

This restriction applies during the term of discussions and for **3 years thereafter**.

## 5. No Residual Use

The Recipient agrees that it shall not rely upon, use, or exploit any retained knowledge, memory, or generalised learnings derived from Confidential Information ("residuals") for any purpose outside the agreed engagement with Lucentra.

## 6. Intellectual Property Ownership

All Intellectual Property disclosed remains the sole and exclusive property of Lucentra.

- No license, assignment, or transfer of rights is granted except as explicitly agreed in writing
- All improvements, derivatives, or outputs based on Lucentra's Confidential Information shall belong exclusively to Lucentra
- **Bodhi and any associated systems are explicitly excluded from any implied or future rights, claims, or interests by the Recipient**

## 7. Work Product and Assignment (if engagement proceeds)

To the extent the Parties enter into a commercial agreement:

- All work product, code, designs, and deliverables created by the Recipient in connection with Lucentra (the "Work Product") shall be owned exclusively by Lucentra
- To the extent any such Work Product does not qualify as "work made for hire", the Recipient hereby irrevocably assigns all rights, title, and interest in such Work Product to Lucentra
- The Parties agree that ownership of all Work Product is not contingent upon continued engagement, future payments, or the duration of the relationship
- In the event of termination or cessation of the relationship for any reason, Lucentra shall retain full and unrestricted rights to use, modify, and commercialise all Work Product created up to that point, without any additional payment obligation beyond what is explicitly agreed in writing
- Where not legally possible, the Recipient hereby irrevocably assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work to Lucentra
- The Recipient acknowledges that its compensation is limited to the agreed equity and/or revenue share, and no additional fees, charges, or payments shall be due unless expressly agreed in writing

## 8. Return, Destruction, and Delivery of Work Product

Upon request or termination of discussions, the Recipient shall:

- Immediately cease use of Confidential Information
- Return or securely destroy all Confidential Information belonging to Lucentra, except as required for legal or compliance purposes

Notwithstanding the above, the Recipient shall not destroy any work product, code, designs, or deliverables created for Lucentra ("Work Product").

All Work Product shall be promptly delivered to Lucentra in full, including all source code, assets, documentation, and access credentials, in a usable and complete format. The Recipient shall confirm in writing that:

(a) all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed as required; and
(b) all Work Product has been fully delivered to Lucentra.

## 9. Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for **5 years**, with confidentiality and non-use obligations surviving for that period.

Clauses relating to IP ownership and non-derivation shall **survive indefinitely**.

## 10. Remedies

The Recipient acknowledges that breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm. Lucentra shall be entitled to:

- Injunctive relief (without proof of damages)
- Damages and legal costs
- Any other remedies available at law or equity

## 11. No Obligation

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to proceed with any transaction or engagement.

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
